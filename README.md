One short pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud — 84 characters, under the 200-character cap.

> Are you autocomplete? Because I was just thinking of you before you even said hello.

The twist: autocomplete predicts what you are about to say, so the machine was already thinking of you before you even said hello. Charming, flattering, and free to laugh off — no pressure, no leering.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (and `entry.txt` beside `main.ts`). Deterministic, no network.
